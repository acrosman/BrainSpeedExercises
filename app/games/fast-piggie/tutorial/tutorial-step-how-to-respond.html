After the guinea pigs vanish, the circle stays on screen as empty wedges. Choose the wedge where the orange piggie was.

  • Click the wedge. It is shaded as you move the pointer over it.
  • Or use the arrow keys to move between wedges, then press Enter or Space to choose.
  • If you are wrong, your wedge turns red and the correct wedge is shown in yellow.

The next round starts on its own a moment later.
