After the guinea pigs vanish, the empty circle stays on screen. Pick the spot where the orange piggie was.

  • Click that spot. The slice of the circle under your pointer is shaded so you can see what you are choosing.
  • Or use the arrow keys to move the shaded slice around the circle, then press Enter or Space to choose.
  • A correct answer flashes green. If you are wrong, your slice turns red and the correct slice turns yellow.

The next round starts on its own a moment later.
