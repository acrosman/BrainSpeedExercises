When the cards flip face down, choose the three cards where the striped greyhound was.

  • Click a card, or press Tab to move to it and Enter or Space to choose it.
  • Each greyhound you find stays face up with a green outline, and the Found count goes up.
  • Find all three to complete the round.
  • A wrong card turns red and ends the round. There is no partial credit. The greyhound cards are shown, then you get a new grid at the same level.
