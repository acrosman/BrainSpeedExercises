• Complete 3 rounds in a row without a wrong card to move up a level. The grid grows by one row and one column, and the cards stay face up a little less time.
  • Miss 3 rounds in a row and you drop back two levels.

You start at level 1 with a 3 by 3 grid shown for 1500 milliseconds. You score one point for each greyhound you find. Press End Game whenever you want to stop and save your progress.
