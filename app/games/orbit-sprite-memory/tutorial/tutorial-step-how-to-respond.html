When the last rabbit disappears, a small circle marks every spot where a rabbit appeared. Choose the three spots where the target appeared.

  • Click a spot, or press Tab to move to it and Enter or Space to choose it. A chosen spot fills in blue.
  • Changed your mind? Choose the same spot again to clear it.
  • As soon as you choose a third spot, your answer is checked. There is no Submit button. The round counts only if all three spots are right.
  • The circle flashes green for a correct answer and red for a miss. Then every rabbit returns to its spot for a moment, so you can see where the target was.
