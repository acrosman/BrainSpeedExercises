When the balls stop, choose the balls that were your targets.

  • Click a ball, or press Tab to move to it and Enter or Space to choose it. A chosen ball gets a gold ring.
  • Changed your mind? Choose the same ball again to clear it.
  • As soon as you have chosen as many balls as there were targets, your answer is checked. There is no Submit button.
  • Targets you found get a green ring and targets you missed get a red ring. The round counts only if you find every target.
