• Get 3 rounds right in a row to move up a level. The balls move faster, and every few levels there are more balls, more targets, and a longer time to track them.
  • Get 3 rounds wrong in a row and you drop back two levels.

You start at level 1 with 8 balls, 3 of them targets, moving for 5 seconds. You score one point for each round where you find every target. The speed trend shows how fast the balls moved, so higher is better. Press End Game whenever you want to stop and save your progress.
