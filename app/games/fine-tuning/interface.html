Fine Tuning

How to Play

Pick out which of two quick speech sounds matches the target, even when the sounds differ by a single consonant, such as “ba” and “da”.

  1. Listen to the target sound.
  2. Listen to the two sounds that follow it, one after the other.
  3. Choose First or Second, whichever matched the target. Use the buttons or press 1 or 2.
  4. Get 3 correct in a row to advance to a harder level.
  5. Get 3 wrong in a row to drop back 2 levels.

As you advance, the consonants get quicker, the two sounds come closer together, and background noise is added. Headphones work best. While you play, you can switch between a lower voice, a higher voice, or a mix of both.
