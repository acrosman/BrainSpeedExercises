Every sound is a consonant followed by a vowel. The two choices always share the vowel and differ only in the consonant at the start, so listen closely to the very beginning of each sound.

  • Some pairs differ in where the sound is made: “ba” (lips), “da” (tongue behind the teeth), and “ga” (back of the tongue).
  • Others differ in when the voice starts: “ba” and “da” start right away, while “pa” and “ta” start with a short puff of air.

Order matters: if the target was “ba” and you heard “da” then “ba”, the answer is Second.
