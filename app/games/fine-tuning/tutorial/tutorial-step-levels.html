• You score a point for every correct answer.
  • Get 3 correct in a row to move up a level. Get 3 wrong in a row and you drop back two levels, until you're back where you started.

Each session starts at level 1, where the consonants are slowed down. As you move up:

  • The consonants speed up to a natural pace, then faster than natural.
  • The two sounds come closer together.
  • Background noise is added, and it gets louder.
  • With Mixed voices, the hardest levels speak the target in one voice and the two choices in the other.

The speed trend tracks how long each consonant takes, so lower is better. Press End Game whenever you want to stop and save your progress.
