While the sounds play, the answer buttons are off. Once all three sounds end, the buttons turn on.

  • Click First or Second, whichever matched the target, or press 1 or 2.
  • Not sure? Press Replay to hear the target and both sounds again before you answer. Replaying does not change your score.
  • If you are wrong, the message below the buttons tells you the target and which sound matched it.
