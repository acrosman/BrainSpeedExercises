While the sweeps play, the screen says Listen… and the answer buttons are off. Once both sweeps end, the buttons turn on.

  • Click the sequence you heard, or press its number key: 1 for Up-Up, 2 for Up-Down, 3 for Down-Up, or 4 for Down-Down.
  • Not sure? Press Replay to hear the same pair again before you answer. Replaying does not change your score.
  • If you are wrong, the message below the buttons tells you which sequence played.
