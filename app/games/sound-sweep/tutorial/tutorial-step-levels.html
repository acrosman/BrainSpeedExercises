• You score a point for every correct answer.
  • Get 3 correct in a row to move up a level. Each level makes the sweeps shorter and the gap between them smaller, until they are quick chirps.
  • Get 3 wrong in a row and you drop back two levels, until you're back where you started.

Each session starts at level 1: each sweep lasts 600 milliseconds, with 600 milliseconds of quiet between them.

The speed trend tracks how long each sweep lasts, so lower is better. Press End Game whenever you want to stop and save your progress.
