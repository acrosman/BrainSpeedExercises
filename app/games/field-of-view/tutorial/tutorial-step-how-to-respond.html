After the kitten and toy vanish, a picture of a field covers the grid. Then answer two questions with the controls beside the grid:

  1. Under Which Kitten Appeared, choose the kitten that was in the center.
  2. Under Which Square Was the Toy In, choose the square where the toy was.
  • Click each choice, or press Tab to move to it and Enter or Space to choose it.
  • Answer in either order. Your answer is checked as soon as you have picked both.
  • A round counts as correct only when both answers are right. The grid flashes green for correct and red for wrong.
