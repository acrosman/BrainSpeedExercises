• Get 3 correct in a row and the kitten and toy are shown for a little less time (about 17 milliseconds, one screen refresh).
  • Get 3 wrong in a row and they are shown for a little longer (about 33 milliseconds).
  • Once the display time reaches 300 milliseconds, the grid grows from 3 by 3 to 5 by 5, so the toy can appear farther from the center.

You start at 500 milliseconds. Your threshold is the shortest display time you reach, so lower is better. Press End Game whenever you want to stop and save your progress.
