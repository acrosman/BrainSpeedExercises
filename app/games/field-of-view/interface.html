Field of View

How to Play

A kitten appears in the center while a toy appears on an outer-edge cell. After a brief flash, the field image masks the board.

  1. Track which kitten appeared in the center.
  2. Note where the toy appeared in the outer edge.
  3. When the field mask appears, select the kitten and click the toy location from the controls on the left.
  4. As the game progresses the display time will drop, and the size of the grid will increase.

Photosensitivity warning: Field of View flashes images of a cat and toys on screen for short times. No formal testing of this game has been done to determine if it poses a risk. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or have ever had a seizure, you may want to talk to a doctor before playing. Stop and seek professional advice if you notice anything that could indicate a problem.
