• You score a point for every correct response: each otter you respond to in time, and each fish you stop for.
  • Stop for the fish 3 times in a row, with no mistakes in between, to move up a level. Each level shows every picture for less time, and allows one more otter before the fish.
  • Make 3 mistakes before your next good stop and you drop back two levels, but never below level 1.

Each session starts at level 1: every picture is shown for 1500 milliseconds, and up to 5 otters come before the fish.

In the stats, No-Go Hits counts the times you pressed for the fish, and Avg Resp is your average time to respond to an otter, updated after each fish. The speed trend tracks the Interval, how long each picture is shown, so lower is better. Press End Game whenever you want to stop and save your progress.
