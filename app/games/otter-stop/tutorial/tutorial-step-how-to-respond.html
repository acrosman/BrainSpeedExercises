• To respond to an otter, press Space or click the picture. Respond before the otter disappears. When you do, the next picture comes right away.
  • If an otter disappears before you respond, that is a miss, and you see Too slow!
  • When the fish appears, do nothing until it goes away. You see Great stop! If you press while the fish is showing, you see Oops — too fast!
  • After a mistake, the next picture is always an otter.
